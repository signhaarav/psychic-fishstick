    How it works

    Three steps, no forms, nothing to install. The whole site is static files — that is why it can stay free.

    1. 1

      Pick a subject and chapter

      Search or browse. Every chapter map comes from the NCERT syllabus, so what you see is what your exam asks.

    2. 2

      Read, practise, keep notes

      Lessons and practice sets live on your device. Your progress and notes stay in this browser — no account, nothing uploaded.

    3. 3

      Ask for help, if you want it

      Add your own AI key once and get explanations in plain language. Remove the key any time; the rest of the site keeps working.

    AI help is BYOK — bring your own key

    We do not run a server, so there is nowhere for us to keep a secret key of our own. You paste yours, it is stored only in this browser, and it is sent only to the provider you picked — never to us, never to anyone else. Free tiers exist on most of these.

    • Google Gemini
    • Groq
    • OpenRouter
    • OpenAI
    Everything else works with no key at all.
